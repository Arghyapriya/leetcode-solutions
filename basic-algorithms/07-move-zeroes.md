## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)
**Link:**(https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/)

### Approach
Used a two-pointer technique to iterate through the array, shifting all non-zero elements forward and filling the remaining positions with zeros.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(n)$
- Space: $O(1)$

### Notes
Handled edge cases like an array containing all zeros or no zeros at all.
