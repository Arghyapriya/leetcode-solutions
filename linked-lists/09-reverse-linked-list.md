## Problem: Reverse Linked List (Easy)
**Link:** (https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/)

### Approach
Used an iterative approach with three pointers (`prev`, `current`, and `next`) to reverse the direction of the pointers in-place.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(n)$
- Space: $O(1)$

### Notes
Handled edge cases like an empty list or a list with only a single node.
