## Problem: Valid Parentheses (Easy)
**Link:** (https://leetcode.com/problems/valid-parentheses/)

### Approach
Used a stack data structure to push opening brackets and pop/match them whenever a corresponding closing bracket was encountered.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(n)$
- Space: $O(n)$

### Notes
Handled edge cases like strings with odd lengths or starting with a closing bracket.
